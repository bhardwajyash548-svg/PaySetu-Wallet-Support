PaySetu Wallet Help Docs
PaySetu Wallet is a made-up business. This document is practice data for a learning project.
PaySetu Wallet is a made-up UPI payments app with a small wallet, bill payments and cashback offers.

Frequently asked questions

Q: How do I send money?
A: Tap Send, enter a UPI ID, phone number or scan a QR code, enter the amount and confirm with your UPI PIN.

Q: What is the daily UPI limit?
A: ₹1,00,000 a day and up to 20 transactions a day. Some banks set lower limits.

Q: My payment failed but money was deducted.
A: Failed payments are reversed to your bank automatically within 3 working days.

Q: My payment is pending. What should I do?
A: Wait up to 48 hours. A pending payment either completes or is reversed on its own.

Q: I sent money to the wrong UPI ID. Can you reverse it?
A: We cannot reverse a payment sent to a valid UPI ID. Ask the person to return it, and raise a dispute within 30 days if they don't.

Q: What is the PaySetu wallet?
A: A prepaid balance for quick payments, bills and recharges. Add money by UPI, debit card or credit card.

Q: How much can I keep in my wallet?
A: Up to ₹10,000 without full KYC and up to ₹2,00,000 with full KYC.

Q: How do I complete KYC?
A: Go to Profile, tap KYC and verify with your PAN and Aadhaar OTP. It usually takes a few minutes.

Q: Can I move wallet money to my bank?
A: Yes, only with full KYC. A 2% fee applies.

Q: Which bills can I pay?
A: Electricity, gas, water, broadband, DTH, postpaid mobile, FASTag and credit card bills.

Q: I paid a bill but the provider still shows it unpaid.
A: Providers can take up to 48 hours to update. Keep the payment receipt from the app.

Q: When will I get cashback?
A: Cashback is added to your wallet within 24 hours of an eligible payment.

Q: Will PaySetu ever ask for my PIN or OTP?
A: Never. PaySetu never asks for your UPI PIN, OTP, CVV or full card number on call, chat or SMS.

Q: I think my account is hacked. What do I do?
A: Tap Block account in Settings at once and call the cybercrime helpline 1930. Then report it to support.

Q: How do I change my UPI PIN?
A: Go to Bank accounts, choose the account and tap Change UPI PIN. You need your debit card details.

Q: How do I add a bank account?
A: Go to Bank accounts, tap Add and pick your bank. Your phone number must be linked to that bank.

Q: I changed my phone. How do I log in?
A: Install the app, verify your number with an OTP and set up UPI again with your debit card.

Q: When will a merchant refund reach me?
A: Merchant refunds reach the source account in 5 to 7 working days after the merchant starts them.

Q: How long does a dispute take?
A: Disputes are reviewed within 7 working days. You get updates by SMS.

Q: Are there fees for UPI payments?
A: No fees for UPI payments from a bank account. Adding money by credit card has a 2% fee.

Policies

Policy: Failed, pending and wrong payments policy
- Failed payments are reversed to the bank automatically within 3 working days.
- Pending payments complete or are reversed within 48 hours.
- Payments to a valid but wrong UPI ID cannot be reversed by PaySetu. A dispute can be raised within 30 days.
- Disputes are reviewed within 7 working days.

Policy: Account security and fraud policy
- PaySetu never asks for a UPI PIN, OTP, CVV or full card or bank account number.
- If an account may be hacked, the user should tap Block account, call the cybercrime helpline 1930, and report it to support at once.
- Transaction details are shared only with the account holder.

Policy: Wallet and KYC policy
- Wallet limit without full KYC: ₹10,000. With full KYC: ₹2,00,000.
- Full KYC needs PAN and Aadhaar OTP verification.
- Wallet to bank transfers need full KYC and carry a 2% fee.
- Adding money by credit card carries a 2% fee.
